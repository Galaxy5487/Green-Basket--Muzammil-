Our Story

It started with one overfull basket.

In 2016, our founder Meera was helping her family sell surplus vegetables at a weekend market. The produce was exceptional — but by the time it reached city shelves, most of it had lost its shine.

So we tried something simple: deliver it ourselves, the same day it was picked. One basket became ten, ten became a hundred, and GreenBasket was born.

  • Direct partnerships with small and mid-sized farms
  • Fair, transparent pricing for growers and customers
  • Shorter supply chains, dramatically less food waste

Process

From farm to your kitchen

Four careful steps, completed within a single day.

01

Harvested at Dawn

Farmers pick to order each morning, so nothing is cut before it's needed.

02

Sorted & Checked

Every crate is graded by hand — size, colour, firmness and cleanliness.

03

Packed Airtight

Breathable crates and plant-based liners keep moisture in and bruising out.

04

Delivered Next Morning

Routed locally so your basket never spends a night in a warehouse.

Farmer Partnership

We buy what the farm grows — not what a shelf demands.

Our growers are partners, not suppliers. We agree on a fair price before the season starts, so a bad week of weather doesn't mean a bad month of income.

In return, they grow for flavour rather than shelf life — which is exactly why our vegetables taste the way they do.

  • Pre-agreed seasonal pricing with every partner farm
  • Support for organic certification and soil regeneration
  • Surplus produce redirected to community kitchens
